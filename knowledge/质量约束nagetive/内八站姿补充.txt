For an explicitly requested inward-toe standing pose only: preserve the intended inward-toe silhouette and a small natural space between the legs. Show gentle coordinated rotation from the hips, softly bent knees, continuous thigh-knee-calf contours and knee directions consistent with the corresponding feet. Avoid forcing knees to meet, a sharp sideways hinge at a knee, a twisted planted ankle or changing the requested stance into a crossed-leg or staggered pose. Keep credible weight-bearing contact. Do not apply this standing-only instruction to sitting or lying poses.
