1. 垂臂并腿悬浮
姿势：身体离开承托面悬浮，躯干保持直立，肩膀放松，双臂向两侧斜下方展开，与躯干约呈二十至三十度夹角；手肘略松，手指自然舒展。双腿靠拢并向下伸展，双膝仅轻微放松，双脚离开承托面且不交叉，脚踝保持自然角度。头部微转，视线平静看向前方。
英文提示词参考：Hover with the entire body clear of the supporting surface. Keep the torso upright and shoulders relaxed. Extend both arms diagonally downward and outward, about twenty to thirty degrees from the torso, with soft elbows and naturally relaxed fingers. Keep both legs close together and extended downward with only a slight knee bend. Both feet remain airborne, uncrossed, with neutral ankles. Turn the head slightly and look calmly forward.

2. 侧身屈膝起飞
姿势：身体整体腾空，以侧面或四分之三侧面展示，躯干轻微前倾，背部与骨盆保持自然连接，不折腰。双臂在身体两侧向斜下方展开，手肘放松，手掌自然朝内。双腿相近，一条腿向下伸展，另一条腿的膝盖轻微弯曲，使两只离地的脚略有前后高低差；不高抬腿、不交叉扭膝。头部顺着身体朝向微抬，视线看向前方。
英文提示词参考：Lift the whole body into the air, shown in profile or three-quarter side view. Lean the torso only slightly forward, keeping the spine and pelvis naturally aligned. Hold both arms diagonally down and out beside the body, elbows relaxed and palms turned naturally inward. Keep the thighs close; extend one leg downward and bend the other knee gently so the airborne feet have a small fore-and-aft and height offset. Avoid a high leg lift or twisted crossed knees. Raise the chin slightly and look ahead along the direction of the body.
