1. 下腰站立
姿势：侧面或轻微三分侧站立，髋部向后移并从髋折叠，使上身明显前倾；腰背保持自然轻弧而非向后下腰。双腿自然伸展、膝轻微放松，两脚相隔不大且略有前后错位，双足稳定承重。双臂向下伸，左右手掌各轻扶同侧膝盖上方的大腿前侧，手指自然向下，肘微松。颈部自然伸展、下巴轻抬，视线朝侧前方，肩膀放松；不强行扭腰或过度压膝。
英文提示词参考：Standing in side or slight three-quarter view, hinge forward at the hips while shifting the pelvis gently backward. Torso leans clearly forward with a natural mild back curve, not a backward gymnastic backbend. Legs extend naturally with soft knees; feet remain close with a slight stagger and stable support. Both arms extend downward, each palm lightly resting on the front of its own thigh just above the knee, fingers naturally directed downward and elbows relaxed. Extend the neck naturally, lift the chin slightly and look toward the side-front. Relax the shoulders; no extreme lumbar twist, locked knees or forced joint rotation.
